### Fill in the Blank

1. The Calvin cycle occurs in the __________.  
   Answer: stroma

2. The plant hormone often associated with fruit ripening is __________.  
   Answer: ethylene

### Short Answer

Explain why closing stomata can help a plant survive drought but may reduce photosynthesis.

Suggested answer: Closing stomata reduces water loss through transpiration, which helps preserve water during drought. However, it also limits carbon dioxide entry into the leaf, which can slow the Calvin cycle and reduce sugar production.
