# Introduction to Plant Biology - Fictional Past Exam

> Fictional demo exam for software testing. This is not a real school exam and is not copied from copyrighted teaching material.

Time limit: 90 minutes  
Total points: 100

## Part A: Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer.

1. Which structure is most directly responsible for regulating gas exchange in leaves?
   A. Root hair  
   B. Stoma  
   C. Xylem vessel  
   D. Seed coat

2. In photosynthesis, oxygen released by plants mainly comes from:
   A. Carbon dioxide split in the Calvin cycle  
   B. Glucose breakdown in mitochondria  
   C. Water split during light-dependent reactions  
   D. Nitrogen fixation in roots

3. Which tissue primarily transports sugars from source leaves to sink tissues?
   A. Phloem  
   B. Xylem  
   C. Epidermis  
   D. Cork

4. A plant shoot bends toward a window because:
   A. Ethylene accumulates in the roots  
   B. Auxin distribution causes unequal cell elongation  
   C. Xylem transports sugar toward the light  
   D. Guard cells permanently close

5. Which hormone is most closely associated with drought response and stomatal closure?
   A. Gibberellin  
   B. Cytokinin  
   C. Abscisic acid  
   D. Auxin

## Part B: Fill in the Blank

1. The two main vascular tissues in plants are __________ and __________.
2. The Calvin cycle takes place in the __________ of the chloroplast.
3. A seed begins active growth during the process called __________.
4. The waxy outer layer that helps reduce water loss from leaves is the __________.
5. Cells that open and close stomata are called __________ cells.

## Part C: Short Answer

1. Explain why stomata create a trade-off between photosynthesis and water conservation.

2. Compare xylem and phloem in terms of what they transport and their general direction of movement.

3. Describe the difference between pollination and fertilization in flowering plants.

4. A student places seedlings in a box with light entering from one side. After three days, the shoots bend toward the opening. Propose a simple explanation using plant hormones.

## Part D: Data Interpretation

A fictional experiment measured the number of open stomata per leaf sample under two conditions.

| Condition | Average open stomata per field of view |
| --- | ---: |
| Well-watered | 42 |
| Drought stress | 11 |

1. State the trend shown in the table.
2. Which hormone may be involved in this response?
3. Explain how this response could help the plant survive.

## Part E: Essay

Choose one prompt.

1. Describe how plant structure supports life on land. Your answer should include at least three of the following: roots, stems, leaves, vascular tissue, cuticle, stomata, or seeds.

2. Explain how energy and matter move through photosynthesis. Your answer should include the light-dependent reactions, the Calvin cycle, ATP, NADPH, carbon dioxide, and oxygen.
