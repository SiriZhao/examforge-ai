# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in RecallForge AI welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or technical level.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them thoughtfully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, unwelcome contact, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
